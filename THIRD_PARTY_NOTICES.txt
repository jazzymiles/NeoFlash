NeoFlash — adapted ActionScript algorithms

The selected Rust String and geometry algorithms contain portions adapted from
Ruffle. Matrix rotation also retains the upstream OpenFL attribution.
No complete Ruffle or OpenFL engine is included. The MIT option is selected for
these portions. See docs/AS2-REUSE-PROVENANCE.md and docs/AS3-REUSE-PROVENANCE.md
for pinned revisions, exact source paths and modifications.
